Scope: canonical apw-meister.kicad_sch and its assigned component/footprint

1. HIGH: PSU enable does not have a guaranteed default-off state.

J2.5 PSU_EN connects directly to U3 GPIO25. The local PSU_PICO.md identifies
enable as active-low; RP2040 GPIO25 defaults to a weak pull-down during reset.
There is no external circuit establishing off during reset/boot. An APW12
internal pull-up might dominate, so accidental turn-on is a risk, not a
measured outcome. Original bitcrane main.rs also initializes GPIO25 low.
For a USB adapter, establish and test the desired default-off behavior during
reset, BOOTSEL, USB removal, firmware crash, and independent PSU power-up.
A normally-off open-drain transistor with a gate pull-down and a verified
PSU-side pull-up is one possible implementation; account for its inversion
in firmware. Do not assume a pull-up to USB-derived 3V3 solves USB removal.

2. HIGH / UNRESOLVED: APW12 electrical interface needs hardware validation.

SDA, SCL, and enable connect directly to RP2040 pins. Check the intended PSU
revision's idle voltages and pull-ups with USB both powered and disconnected.
The RP2040 is not 5 V tolerant. GPIO14/15/25 are fault-tolerant digital pins,
however: its datasheet describes little input current below 3.63 V with IOVDD
off. The GP28/ADC back-power warning in the Pico adapter documentation must
not be transferred indiscriminately to this GPIO25 design.

No external SDA/SCL pull-ups are fitted. Reference firmware explicitly enables
internal pull-ups and bit-bangs at 400 Hz, despite a stale 400 kHz comment in
main.rs. This can explain operation without board pull-ups; it does not
validate normal-speed I2C. Reserve optional pull-up footprints, then select
values against actual PSU pull-ups, bus capacitance, voltage, and speed.

3. MEDIUM / CONFIRMED: USB series resistors differ from RP2040 requirements.

R2 and R3 are 33 ohms. Raspberry Pi specifies 27 ohms on both USB data lines.
Change both values and their exact purchasing identifiers to 27-ohm parts;
place them near RP2040 during layout. 33 ohms does not prove USB will fail,
but is an avoidable departure from the specified termination.

4. MEDIUM: Crystal network is not a validated reference implementation.

Y1 C9002 / X322512MSB4SI is a 12 MHz, 20 pF-load crystal with 80-ohm maximum
ESR. C2/C4 are each 30 pF, giving 15 pF plus stray capacitance. Thus 20 pF
requires about 5 pF of effective stray capacitance, which is not established.
There is no XOUT series damping resistor. Use a characterized crystal/load/
damping combination, or validate startup and drive over voltage/temperature.
The Raspberry Pi reference uses ABM8-272-T3 (10 pF load, 50-ohm ESR), 15 pF
capacitors and 1 kohm series resistance at 3.3 V. Do not blindly transfer the
1 kohm value to a different crystal without checking startup margin.

5. MEDIUM: LED supply bypassing is incomplete.

U1 has no dedicated local bypass capacitor. C17 is shared with the regulator
input and is not a substitute for deliberate LED-local placement. Add a
100 nF ceramic at U1 VDD/GND and consider the manufacturer's suggested DIN
series protection resistor. The exact SK6812SIDE-A-RVS datasheet specifies
VIH(min) = 2.6 V at 5 V: direct 3.3 V logic is supported; the generic 0.7*VDD
threshold sometimes attributed to addressable LEDs is not applicable here.

6. MEDIUM: BOM fields disagree about several parts.

J1 PARTNO is GCT USB4105-GF-A, whereas C2988369 is G-Switch GT-USB-7010ASV.
The two drawings agree on the principal contact, locating-hole and mounting
stake positions used by the footprint. This is an identity/approved-alternate
issue, not a demonstrated pad mismatch. G-Switch specifies a 0.8 mm board in
its drawing; confirm the intended board thickness/mechanical fit when choosing
the actual connector. Make MPN, manufacturer, supplier code and footprint
describe the selected part consistently.

SW2/SW3 show GT-TC029B-H025-L1N but PARTNO and footprint specify CS1213AGF260.
Both are DNP and excluded from BOM. Confirm that deliberately omitting boot/
reset switches matches the initial programming/recovery plan (SWD is on USB-C
SBU contacts). R4/R5 similarly mix a Stackpole MPN with Uniroyal LCSC codes,
although the latter are electrically suitable 5.1 kohm resistors.
J2 should gain explicit HCTL / HC-PHD-2*3A identity fields.

7. CONNECTOR DOCUMENTATION: preserve the reference's custom numbering.

The J2 footprint is physically identical to bitcrane J10, but its numbering
is row-sequential rather than the alternating numbering in HCTL's drawing.
With the footprint's pin 1 at lower right, its rows are:

    6  5  4       upper row, local y = -1 mm
    3  2  1       lower row, local y = +1 mm

HCTL's corresponding drawing numbers those positions:

    6  4  2
    5  3  1

Therefore the numeric signal list in the schematic documentation is a
project-specific convention. It matches bitcrane; this review does not
establish a physical wiring error. Add a keyed mating/board-view diagram and
continuity-check a real cable. Do not substitute a conventionally numbered
footprint without remapping the schematic to preserve physical connectivity.
The generic Odd_Even symbol does not communicate this custom numbering.

8. SOURCING / ROBUSTNESS FOLLOW-UP.

JLC's live lookup returned stock 0 for U2 C2843335 / W25Q16JVUXIQ on the review
date. The flash is electrically appropriate, but select an available exact
package/order-code option before assembly. Its IQ suffix has quad enable fixed
on. QSPI_SD3 is an active data line: reject the generic audit suggestion to add
a 100 nF reset capacitor there. Its unusual 1.6 x 0.2 mm central pad agrees
with the Winbond UX package drawing; it is not an obvious footprint error.

There is no explicit ESD protection on USB or APW signal contacts. Consider
low-capacitance USB protection and suitable connector-side APW protection for
a frequently handled cable adapter. This is a robustness recommendation,
not evidence of a DC connectivity failure.

Checks that passed and limits

APW GPIO assignments and the reference's physical connector mapping agree.
No power rail is exported to the APW connector. Test pads 1/4 remain isolated
from MCU signals. AP2112K-3.3 pin mapping, voltage range, and nominal 1 uF
input/output capacitance meet its datasheet guidance; final effective capacitor
values and thermal behavior remain layout/load checks. Flash supply/pinout,
RP2040 power decoupling, USB-C CC resistors and data-pair duplication appear
consistent. No definitive flash, regulator, or LED logic-level incompatibility
was found.

Konnect geometric checks found no shorted nets, single-pin nets, or orphan
items. Independent ERC reports 0 errors / 17 inherited warnings, principally
unspecified legacy pin types and unavailable library entries. These should be
cleaned up for maintainability. The selected Backplane KiCad build still
produces the previously documented contradictory net merges and ERC error;
this is not a clean Backplane ERC sign-off. See apw-meister-schematic.md for
the baseline comparison. Automated decoupling/power audits are net-based and
do not establish placement quality; their generic recommendations were
